Manifesto

Five slightly unhinged friends. One board.

Most assistants are built to be rational, agreeable and predictable. Dealing does the opposite: five agents who think differently, argue with each other and attack your problem from five sides.

Marijuana connects ideas nobody else would. Ayahuasca asks if you're solving the right problem. Alcohol says what everyone is thinking. LSD bends the frame. Cocaine just gets it done.

Then the Chairman weighs every argument, remembers what matters to you and lands on one decision. The board researches, drafts, negotiates and acts. Anything consequential waits for your OK.

For everything you'd normally send to the group chat

  • DatingThis guy just texted me. I have no idea what to say. Dealing, take over.
  • ArgumentsI'm fighting with someone. Here's the conversation. Handle it.
  • ShoppingI want this, but it's overpriced. Negotiate a better deal.
  • SocialI don't want to go to this dinner anymore. Get me out without drama.
  • BoredomI have $40 and three free hours. Figure something out.
  • OverthinkingI've rewritten this message 17 times. Just decide for me.
